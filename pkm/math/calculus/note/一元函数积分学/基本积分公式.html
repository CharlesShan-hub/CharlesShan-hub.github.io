基本积分公式

  1. 考凑微分法： $$ \int x_k dx = \frac{1}{k+1}x^{k=1} + C,(k≠-1)\ $$

$$ \int \frac{1}{x^2}dx = -\frac{1}{x}+C\ \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}dx = 2\sqrt{x}+C $$

  1. 求导时“视绝对值而不见” $$ \int \frac{1}{x} = \ln|x|+C $$

  2. e与ln $$ \int e^x dx = e^x+C\ \int a^x dx = a^x/\ln a + C $$

  3. 三角函数

$$ \int \sin x dx =-\cos x+C\ \int \cos x dx =\sin x+C\ \int \tan x dx =\int \frac{\sin x}{\cos x}dx=-\int \frac{d\cos x}{\cos x}= -\ln|\cos x|+C\ \int \cot x dx =\int \frac{\cos x}{\sin x}dx=\int \frac{d\sin x}{\sin x}= \ln|\sin x|+C\ \int \frac{dx}{\cos x}=\int \sec x dx = \ln|\sec x+\tan x|+C\ \int \frac{dx}{\sin x}=\int \csc x dx = \ln|\csc x-\cot x|+C\ \int \sec^2 xdx = \tan x+C\ \int \csc^2 xdx = -\cot x+C\ \int \sec x\tan xdx = \sec x+c\ \int \csc x\cot x dx= -\csc x+C $$

  1. arctan x $$ \int \frac{1}{1+x^2}dx = \arctan x+C\ \int \frac{1}{a^2+x^2} dx = \frac{1}{a}\arctan {\frac{x}{a}}+C $$

  2. arcsin x $$ \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}dx=\arcsin x+C\ \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{a^2-x^2}}dx=\arcsin \frac{x}{a}+C\ $$

  3. 构造1（重要！） $$ \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}dx=\ln(x+\sqrt{x^2+a^2})=C\ \int \frac{1}{\sqrt{x^2-a^2}}dx=\ln|x+\sqrt{x^2-a^2}|=C,|x|>|a| $$

  4. 构造2（重要！） $$ \int \frac{1}{x^2-a^2}dx=\frac{1}{2a}\ln|\frac{x-a}{x+a}|+C\ \int \frac{1}{a^2-x^2}dx=\frac{1}{2a}\ln|\frac{x+a}{x-a}|+C $$

  5. 构造3(重要!!!) $$ \int \sqrt{a^2-x^2}dx=\frac{a^2}{2}\arcsin\frac{x}{a}+\frac{x}{2}\sqrt{a^2-x^2}+C $$

  6. 三角函数扩展 $$ \int \sin^2xdx=\frac{x}{2}-\frac{\sin 2x}{4}+C\ \int \cos^2xdx=\frac{x}{2}+\frac{\sin 2x}{4}+C\ \int \tan^2xdx=\int \sec^2x-1\ dx=\tan x-x+C\ \int \cot^2xdx=\int \csc^2x-1\ dx=-\cot x-x+C $$
