傅里叶级数

  • 标准形式 $$ f(x)\approx S(x) = \frac{a_0}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n\cos \frac{n\pi}{l} x+ b_n\sin \frac{n\pi}{l} x\ a_n = \frac{1}{l}\int_{-l}^{l}f(x)\cos \frac{n\pi}{l} xdx\ b_n = \frac{1}{l}\int_{-l}^{l}f(x)\cos \frac{n\pi}{l} xdx\ $$

  • f(x)是奇函数~正弦级数 $$ a_n = 0\ b_n = \frac{2}{l}\int_{0}^{l}f(x)\sin \frac{n\pi}{l} xdx\ f(x) = \sum_{n=1}^\infty b_n\sin\frac{n\pi}{l}x $$

  • f(x)是偶函数~余弦函数 $$ a_n = \frac{2}{l}\int_{0}^{l}f(x)\cos \frac{n\pi}{l} xdx\ b_n = 0\ f(x) = a_0+\sum_{n=1}^\infty a_n\cos\frac{n\pi}{l}x $$

  • 奇延拓：展开成正弦级数
