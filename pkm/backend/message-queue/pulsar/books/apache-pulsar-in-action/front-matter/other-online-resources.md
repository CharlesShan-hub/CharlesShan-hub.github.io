## Other online resources

Need additional help?

需要更多帮助？

- The Apache Pulsar project website, https://pulsar.apache.org, is a good source of information about the configuration settings of various components of the Apache Pulsar software, as well as various cookbooks on how to implement specific features of the software, and it will have the most current information.
  Apache Pulsar 项目网站 https://pulsar.apache.org 是一个很好的信息来源，可查到 Apache Pulsar 软件各组件的配置项，以及关于如何实现该软件各项具体特性的各种实用指南；并且它会拥有最新的信息。

- The Apache Pulsar Slack channel, apache-pulsar.slack.com, is an active forum where members of the Apache Pulsar community from around the world meet to exchange advice, share best practices, and provide troubleshooting advice to people who are experiencing problems with Pulsar. It is a great place to go for advice if you get stuck.
  Apache Pulsar 的 Slack 频道 apache-pulsar.slack.com 是一个活跃的论坛：来自世界各地的 Apache Pulsar 社区成员在此聚首，交流建议、分享最佳实践，并向那些在使用 Pulsar 时遇到问题的人提供排障建议。如果你卡住了，这是个寻求帮助的好去处。

- In my current capacity as a Developer Advocate, I will continue to develop additional educational content including blog posts and code examples that will be made readily available online at my company’s website, streamnative.io.
  以我目前开发者布道师的身份，我将继续开发更多的教育内容，包括博客文章和代码示例；它们会被方便地发布在公司的网站 streamnative.io 上。
