# acknowledgments
In the order of nature, we cannot render benefits to those from whom we receive them, or only seldom. But the benefit we receive must be rendered again, line for line, deed for deed, cent for cent, to somebody.

按照自然的秩序，我们无法把好处回报给那些我们受其恩惠的人，或者至多只能偶尔为之。但我们所领受的这份好处，必须一报还一报地再付出去——一行对一行，一事对一事，一分对一分——付给某个人。

—Ralph Waldo Emerson

——拉尔夫·沃尔多·爱默生

I want to take this opportunity to thank everyone who contributed in some way to making this book a reality and to acknowledge the fact that I never would have been able to take on such an enormous project without those who helped lay the foundations for it. In the spirit of Emerson, please consider this book my way of paying forward all the knowledge and encouragement you have given me.

我想借这个机会，感谢所有以某种方式为本书得以成真做出过贡献的人，并且承认这样一个事实：如果没有那些为它打下地基的人，我绝不可能承担起如此庞大的一个项目。本着爱默生的精神，请把这本书看作我把你们给予我的全部知识与鼓励继续传递下去的方式。

I would be remiss if I didn’t start this list with the very first person who introduced me to the wonderful world of coding at the very young age of 6: my elementary school principal, Mr. Rodgers, who decided to put me in front of a computer rather than in detention for not paying attention during first-grade math class. You introduced me to the pure creative joy of coding and set me on a lifelong path of learning.

如果我不以这第一个人来开启这份名单，那就是我的疏忽：在我年仅 6 岁时，是他把我带进了编程这个奇妙的世界——我的小学校长 Rodgers 先生。当时我因为一年级数学课上不专心听讲而要被留堂，他却决定把我放到一台电脑前，而不是关进留堂教室。是你让我领略到编程那种纯粹而富有创造力的快乐，并把我送上了一条终身学习的道路。

I also want to acknowledge the development team at Yahoo! that created Pulsar: you wrote an amazing piece of software and contributed it to the open source community for the rest of us to enjoy. Without you this book wouldn’t be possible.

我同样想要感谢 Yahoo! 那个创造了 Pulsar 的开发团队：你们写出了一款了不起的软件，并把它贡献给了开源社区，让我们其余的人得以享用。没有你们，这本书就不可能存在。

I want to acknowledge all my former colleagues at Streamlio, especially Jerry Peng, Ivan Kelly, Matteo Meril, Sijie Guo, and Sanjeev Kulkarni for serving as Apache PMC Members on either Apache Pulsar, Apache BookKeeper, or both. Without your guidance and commitment, Pulsar wouldn’t be where it is today. I also want to thank my former CEO, Karthik Ramasamy, for helping me grow the Apache Pulsar community while working for him at Streamlio: I really appreciate your mentorship.

我要感谢我在 Streamlio 的所有前同事，尤其是 Jerry Peng、Ivan Kelly、Matteo Meril、Sijie Guo 和 Sanjeev Kulkarni——他们在 Apache Pulsar 或 Apache BookKeeper（或两者）担任 Apache PMC 成员。没有你们的指引与投入，Pulsar 不会走到今天这个位置。我还要感谢我的前任首席执行官 Karthik Ramasamy：在 Streamlio 为他工作期间，他帮助我把 Apache Pulsar 社区发展壮大——我非常感激你的提携。

I want to thank all my former colleagues at Splunk for your efforts on getting Apache Pulsar integrated into such a large organization and for helping us promote its adoption across the organization. When presented with a new technology, you stepped up to the plate and did everything you could to make that effort a success. I want to give a special thank you to the connectors team, particularly Alamusi, Gimi, Alex, and Spike.

我要感谢我在 Splunk 的所有前同事，感谢你们为把 Apache Pulsar 整合进如此庞大一个组织所付出的努力，也感谢你们帮助我们在整个组织内推动它的采用。当一项新技术摆在面前时，你们挺身而出，尽其所能地让这番努力取得成功。我要特别感谢连接器团队，尤其是 Alamusi、Gimi、Alex 和 Spike。

I’d also like to thank the reviewers who took time from their busy lives to read my manuscript at various stages of its development. Your positive feedback was a welcomed reassurance that I was on the right track and lifted my spirits when the writing process had worn me down. Your negative feedback was always constructive and provided a fresh perspective on the material that only a fresh set of eyes can provide. This feedback was invaluable to me and ultimately resulted in the book being much better than it would have been without you. Thank you all: Alessandro Campeis, Alexander Schwartz, Andres Sacco, Andy Keffalas, Angelo Simone Scotto, Chris Viner, Emanuele Piccinelli, Eric Platon, Giampiero Granatella, Gianluca Righetto, Gilberto Taccari, Henry Saputra, Igor Savin, Jason Rendel, Jeremy Chen, Kabeer Ahmed, Kent Spillner, Richard Tobias, Sanket Naik, Satej Kumar Sah, Simone Sguazza, and Thorsten Weber.

我还要感谢各位审稿人——你们从繁忙的生活中挤出时间，在我的手稿处于不同开发阶段时阅读它。你们的正面反馈是一种受欢迎的安慰，让我确信自己走在正确的道路上，并且在写作过程把我消磨得疲惫不堪时提振了我的精神。你们的负面反馈始终是建设性的，并且为这些材料提供了只有一双新鲜的眼睛才能给出的新视角。这些反馈对我来说无比宝贵，并最终让这本书比没有你们时要好得多。谢谢大家：Alessandro Campeis、Alexander Schwartz、Andres Sacco、Andy Keffalas、Angelo Simone Scotto、Chris Viner、Emanuele Piccinelli、Eric Platon、Giampiero Granatella、Gianluca Righetto、Gilberto Taccari、Henry Saputra、Igor Savin、Jason Rendel、Jeremy Chen、Kabeer Ahmed、Kent Spillner、Richard Tobias、Sanket Naik、Satej Kumar Sah、Simone Sguazza 和 Thorsten Weber。

To all the online reviewers, thank you for taking the time to provide valuable feedback to me via Manning’s online forum—especially Chris Latimer, and his uncanny knack for finding all the misspellings and grammatical errors that Microsoft Word could not. All future readers owe you a debt of gratitude.

致所有在线审稿人：感谢你们花时间通过 Manning 的在线论坛向我提供宝贵的反馈——尤其是 Chris Latimer，以及他那种不可思议的本领：能把所有 Microsoft Word 找不出来的拼写错误和语法错误全都揪出来。所有未来的读者都欠你们一份感激。

Last, and certainly not least, I want to thank my editors at Manning, especially Karen Miller, Ivan Martinović, Adriana Sabo, Alain Couniot, and Ninoslav Čerkez. Thank you for working with me and for being patient when things got rough. It was a long process, and I couldn’t have done it without your encouragement. Your commitment to the quality of this book has made it better for everyone who reads it. Thanks as well to all the other folks at Manning who worked with me on the production and promotion of the book. It was truly a team effort.

最后，而且绝不仅是最后一点，我要感谢我在 Manning 的各位编辑，尤其是 Karen Miller、Ivan Martinović、Adriana Sabo、Alain Couniot 和 Ninoslav Čerkez。感谢你们与我共事，也感谢你们在事情变得艰难时保持耐心。这是一个漫长的过程，没有你们的鼓励我是不可能完成的。你们对本书品质的执着，让它对每一位读者都变得更好。同样感谢 Manning 所有其他参与本书制作与推广工作的同仁。这真的是一次团队努力。
