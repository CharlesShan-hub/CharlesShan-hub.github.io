2.3.1 Data retention

However, Pulsar also supports namespace-level retention policies that allow you to override this default behavior for situations where you want to retain the topic data for a longer period, such as if you want to access the topic data at a later point in time via the reader interface or SQL.

不过，Pulsar 也支持命名空间级别的保留策略，使你能够在希望更长时间保留主题数据的场合覆盖这一默认行为，例如你希望日后通过 reader 接口或 SQL 来访问这些主题数据。

These retention policies dictate how long you retain messages in persistent storage after they have been acknowledged as consumed by all the known consumers. Acknowledged messages that are not covered by the retention policy will be deleted. Retention policies are defined by a combination of size and time limits and are applied on a per-topic basis to every topic in that namespace. For instance, if you specify a size limit of 100 GB, then up to 100 GB worth of data will be retained in each topic within that namespace, and once this size limit is exceeded, messages will be purged from the topic (from oldest to newest) until the total data volume is under the specified limit again. Similarly, if you specify a time limit of 24 hours, then acknowledged messages for all the topics in the namespace will be retained for a maximum of 24 hours based on the time they were received by the broker.

这些保留策略决定了：在消息被所有已知消费者确认为已消费之后，还要在持久化存储中保留多久。未被保留策略覆盖的已确认消息将被删除。保留策略由大小限制和时间限制共同定义，并以逐主题的方式应用于该命名空间中的每一个主题。举例来说，如果你指定了 100 GB 的大小限制，那么该命名空间内的每个主题最多会保留 100 GB 的数据；一旦超过这个大小限制，消息就会从主题中被清除（从最旧到最新），直到总数据量重新回落到指定限制之下。类似地，如果你指定了 24 小时的时间限制，那么该命名空间中所有主题的已确认消息，将按它们被 broker 接收的时间起算，最多保留 24 小时。

The retention policies require you to specify both a size and a time limit, which are applied independently of one another. Thus, if a message violates either of these limits, it will be removed from the topic, regardless of whether or not it complies with the other policy.

保留策略要求你同时指定大小和时间两个限制，它们彼此独立地生效。因此，如果某条消息违反了这两个限制中的任意一个，它就会被从主题中移除，而不管它是否符合另一条策略。

If you specify a retention policy with time limit of 24 hours and a size limit of 10 GB for the E-payments/refunds namespace, as shown in listing 2.4, then when either of the specified policy limits are reached, the data is deleted. Therefore, it is possible for messages that are less than 24 hours old to be deleted if the total volume exceeds 10 GB.

如果你为 E-payments/refunds 命名空间指定了一个时间限制为 24 小时、大小限制为 10 GB 的保留策略，如清单 2.4 所示，那么当所指定的任一策略限制被触及时，数据就会被删除。因此，如果总数据量超过了 10 GB，那么不到 24 小时的消息也有可能被删除。

Listing 2.4 Setting various Pulsar retention policies

./bin/pulsar-admin namespaces set-retention E-payments/payments \
  --time 24h \
  --size -1                ❶

./bin/pulsar-admin namespaces set-retention E-payments/fraud-detection \
  --time -1 \
  --size 20G               ❷

./bin/pulsar-admin namespaces set-retention E-payments/refunds \
  --time 24h \
  --size 10G               ❸

./bin/pulsar-admin namespaces set-retention E-payments/gift-cards \
  --time -1 \
  --size -1                ❹

❶ Retains all messages less than 24 hours old with no restriction on the size

❶ 保留所有不足 24 小时的消息，对大小不作限制

❷ Retains up to 20 GB of messages with no restriction on the time

❷ 最多保留 20 GB 的消息，对时间不作限制

❸ Retains up to 10 GB of messages less than 24 hours old

❸ 最多保留 10 GB、且不足 24 小时的消息

❹ Retains an infinite number of messages

❹ 保留无限量的消息

It is also possible to set infinite size or time by specifying a value of -1 for either of those settings when you create the retention policy and providing it for both settings, effectively creating an infinite retention policy for the namespace. Therefore, be careful when using that policy, as the data will never be removed from the storage layer; be sure you have sufficient storage capacity and/or configure periodic offloading of the data to tiered storage.

也可以通过在创建保留策略时为这两项设置中的任一项指定 -1 来设定无限的大小或时间；如果对两项都指定 -1，实际上就为该命名空间创建了一条无限保留策略。因此，使用该策略时要格外小心，因为数据将永远不会从存储层中被移除；请确保你有足够的存储容量，和/或配置了把数据周期性卸载到分层存储的机制。
