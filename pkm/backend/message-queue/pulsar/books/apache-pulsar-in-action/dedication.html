dedication

To my father, who promised to read this book even if he can’t understand a word of it. May your heart be filled with pride every time you tell your friends that your son is a published author.

献给我的父亲——他答应过要读一读这本书，哪怕他一个字也看不懂。愿你每次告诉朋友们"你儿子是个出过书的作者"时，心里都满是自豪。

To my mother, who believed in me when no one else did and worked so hard to ensure that I had every opportunity in life to succeed. Your hard work, faith, and tenacity have been a source of inspiration for me. Thank you for passing these traits down to me, as they have been the foundation of all my achievements including this book.

献给我的母亲——在没有人相信我的时候，是她相信我，并且如此辛勤地操劳，以确保我在人生中拥有每一次成功的机会。你的勤勉、信念与坚韧，一直是我的灵感源泉。感谢你把这一切传承给了我，因为它们是我全部成就的根基，这本书也包括在内。
