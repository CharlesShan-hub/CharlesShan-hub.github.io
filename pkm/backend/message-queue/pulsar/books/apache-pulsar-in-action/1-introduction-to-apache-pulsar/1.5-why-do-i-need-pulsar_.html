1.5 Why do I need Pulsar?

2026-10-08book

If you are just getting started with messaging or streaming data applications, you should definitely consider Apache Pulsar as a core component of your messaging infrastructure. However, it is worth noting that there are several technology options that you can choose from, many of which have become entrenched in the software community. In this section, I will attempt to bring to light some of the scenarios in which Apache Pulsar shines above the rest, as well as clear up some common misconceptions about existing systems and point out some of the challenges users of these systems face.

如果你刚开始接触消息或流式数据应用，那么绝对应该考虑把 Apache Pulsar 作为你消息基础设施的核心组件。不过值得注意的是，你可以选择的技术方案有好几种，其中许多已经在软件社区中根深蒂固。在本节中，我将试图揭示 Apache Pulsar 优于其他方案的一些场景，同时澄清关于现有系统的一些常见误解，并指出这些系统的用户所面临的一些挑战。

Within adoption cycles there are often several misconceptions about the entrenched technology that are perpetuated throughout the user community for a multitude of reasons. It is often an uphill battle to convince yourself and others that you need to replace a technology that sits at the very core of your architecture. It was not until we had the benefit of hindsight that we saw that our traditional database systems were fundamentally incapable of scaling to meet the demands imposed by our ever-increasing data and that we needed to rethink the way we stored and processed data with a framework such as Hadoop. Only after we had transitioned our business analytics platforms from traditional data warehouses to Hadoop-based SQL engines, such as Hive, Tez, and Impala, did we realize that those tools had inadequate response times for the end users who were used to subsecond response times. This gave rise to the rapid adoption of Apache Spark as the technology of choice for big data processing.

在技术采纳的周期中，关于那些根深蒂固的技术，常常存在若干误解，并因种种原因在用户社区中长期流传。要说服自己和他人：你需要替换掉一个位于架构最核心位置的技术，往往是一场艰难的攻坚战。直到事后回顾时我们才看清：面对不断增长的数据所带来的需求，我们的传统数据库系统从根本上就无法扩展以满足它们，而我们需要用 Hadoop 这样的框架重新思考存储和处理数据的方式。也只有在我们把商业分析平台从传统数据仓库迁移到 Hive、Tez 和 Impala 等基于 Hadoop 的 SQL 引擎之后，我们才意识到：对于那些已经习惯了亚秒级响应时间的终端用户来说，这些工具的响应时间是不够的。这促成了 Apache Spark 被迅速采纳为大数据处理的首选技术。

I wanted to highlight these two recent technologies to remind us that we cannot let our affinity for the status quo blind us to issues lurking within our core architectural systems and put forth the notion that we need to rethink our approach to messaging systems, as the incumbent technologies in this space, such as RabbitMQ and Kafka, suffer from key architectural flaws. The team that developed Apache Pulsar at Yahoo! could have easily chosen to adopt one of the existing solutions, but after careful consideration they decided not to do so because they needed a messaging platform that provided capabilities that weren’t available in the existing monolithic technologies that we will discuss in the following sections.

我想强调这两项较近期的技术，是为了提醒我们：不能因为对现状的偏爱，而对潜藏在我们核心架构系统中的问题视而不见；同时也是为了提出这样一个观点——我们需要重新思考处理消息系统的方式，因为该领域中现有的主导技术（如 RabbitMQ 和 Kafka）都存在关键的架构缺陷。在 Yahoo! 开发 Apache Pulsar 的团队本可以轻易选择采纳现有的某个方案，但经过仔细考量，他们决定不这么做，因为他们需要一个能够提供现有单体技术所不具备能力的消息平台，我们将在接下来的小节中讨论这些能力。
