1.5.1 Guaranteed message delivery

2026-10-08book

Because of the data durability mechanism within the platform that we have already covered, Pulsar provides guaranteed message delivery for applications. If a message successfully reaches a Pulsar broker, it will be delivered to all of the topic consumers. To provide such a guarantee requires that non-acknowledged messages are stored in a durable manner until they can be delivered to and acknowledged by consumers. This mode of messaging is commonly called persistent messaging. In Pulsar, a configurable number of copies of all messages is stored and synced on disk.

凭借我们在前面已经介绍过的平台内数据持久性机制，Pulsar 为应用提供了有保证的消息投递。如果一条消息成功抵达某个 Pulsar broker，它就会被投递给该主题的所有消费者。要提供这样的保证，就要求未被确认的消息必须以持久化的方式存储，直到它们能够被投递给消费者并获得确认。这种消息模式通常被称为持久化消息（persistent messaging）。在 Pulsar 中，所有消息都会按可配置的份数存储并同步到磁盘上。

By default, Pulsar message brokers ensure that incoming messages are persisted to disk on the storage layer before acknowledging receipt of the message. These messages are kept in Pulsar’s infinitely scalable storage layer until they are acknowledged, thereby ensuring message delivery.

默认情况下，Pulsar 的 broker 会确保传入的消息在确认收到之前，已经持久化到存储层的磁盘上。这些消息会被保存在 Pulsar 可无限扩展的存储层中，直到它们被确认为止，从而保证消息投递。
