# 1.6.1 Unified messaging systems
You are probably familiar with the mnemonic “keep it simple, stupid,” which is often used to remind architects that there is great value in simple designs and solutions. A system that comprises fewer technologies is easier to deploy, maintain, and monitor. As mentioned earlier, there are two common messaging patterns, and until now, if you wanted to support both messaging styles within your infrastructure, you were required to deploy and maintain two completely different systems.

你大概熟悉"keep it simple, stupid"（保持简单）这条口诀，它常被用来提醒架构师：简单的设计与解决方案具有巨大的价值。一个由更少技术构成的系统，更容易部署、维护和监控。正如前面提到的，存在两种常见的消息模式；而在此之前，如果你想在自己的基础设施中同时支持这两种消息风格，就必须部署并维护两套完全不同的系统。

As a bilingual job board, Zhaopin.com has one of the largest selections of job vacancies in China, including both prominent local and foreign companies. The company has over 2.2 million clients and average daily page views of over 68 million. As the company grew, the challenges of maintaining two separate messaging systems, RabbitMQ for queuing and Apache Kafka for pub-sub, became increasingly difficult. By replacing them both with a single unified messaging platform based on Apache Pulsar, they were able to reduce their operational overhead, infrastructure footprint, and ongoing support costs by half, while meeting their requirements of high durability, high throughput, and low latency.

作为双语招聘平台，智联招聘（Zhaopin.com）拥有中国最大的职位空缺库之一，涵盖知名的本土企业与外国企业。该公司拥有超过 220 万客户，日均页面浏览量超过 6800 万。随着公司的发展，维护两套独立消息系统（用 RabbitMQ 做队列、用 Apache Kafka 做发布-订阅）的难度日益加剧。通过用一个基于 Apache Pulsar 的统一消息平台同时替换掉这两者，他们得以把运维开销、基础设施占用和持续支持成本降低一半，同时满足了高持久性、高吞吐量和低延迟的要求。
