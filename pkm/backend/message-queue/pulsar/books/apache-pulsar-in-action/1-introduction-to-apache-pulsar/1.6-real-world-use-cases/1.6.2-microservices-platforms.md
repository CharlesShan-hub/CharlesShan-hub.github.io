### 1.6.2 Microservices platforms

Narvar provides a supply chain management and customer care platform for e-commerce customers around the world, including order tracking and notifications and seamless returns and customer care. Narvar’s platform helps retailers and brands by processing data and events to ensure timely and accurate communication with their customers to 400 million consumers worldwide.

Narvar 为全球电商客户提供供应链管理与客户服务支持平台，包括订单追踪与通知，以及无缝退货和客户服务。Narvar 的平台通过处理数据与事件，帮助零售商和品牌确保其与全球 4 亿消费者之间的沟通及时而准确。

Prior to Apache Pulsar, Narvar’s platform had been built using a variety of messaging and processing technologies over time—from Kafka to Amazon SQS, Kinesis Streams to Kinesis Firehose, and RabbitMQ to AWS Lambda. As its traffic grew, it became apparent that the growing amount of DevOps and developer support required to maintain and scale these systems was unsustainable. Many of them were not containerized, making infrastructure configuration and management burdensome and requiring frequent manual intervention.

在采用 Apache Pulsar 之前，Narvar 的平台多年来是用各种各样的消息与处理技术搭建起来的——从 Kafka 到 Amazon SQS，从 Kinesis Streams 到 Kinesis Firehose，从 RabbitMQ 到 AWS Lambda。随着其流量增长，维护和扩展这些系统所需的 DevOps 与开发支持工作量不断攀升，这一点变得显而易见且难以为继。其中许多系统并未容器化，使得基础设施的配置与管理成为沉重负担，并且需要频繁的人工干预。

Systems like Kafka—while reliable, popular, and open source—had significant maintenance overhead as they scaled. Increasing throughput required increasing partitions, tuning consumers, and a large amount of manual intervention by developers and DevOps. Similarly, cloud-native solutions like Kinesis Streams and Kinesis Firehose were not cloud-agnostic, making it hard to decouple the choice of cloud solutions from functionality and making it difficult to leverage technologies in other clouds and to support customers who needed to run on other public clouds.

Kafka 这样的系统——虽然可靠、流行且开源——在扩展时有着可观的维护开销。要提升吞吐量，就必须增加分区、调优消费者，并需要开发人员和 DevOps 大量的人工干预。同样地，Kinesis Streams 和 Kinesis Firehose 这类云原生解决方案并不具备云中立性，这使得云解决方案的选择难以与功能解耦，也难以利用其他云上的技术，并难以支持那些需要在其他公有云上运行的客户。

Narvar decided to transition its microservice-based platform over to Apache Pulsar because like Kafka, Pulsar was reliable, cloud-agnostic and open source. Unlike Kafka, Pulsar entailed very little maintenance overhead and scaled with minimal manual intervention. Pulsar was containerized and built on Kubernetes from the outset, making it much more scalable and maintainable. Most importantly for Narvar was Pulsar Functions, which allowed Narvar to develop microservices that consumed and processed the incoming events directly on the messaging system itself, eliminating the need for expensive Lambda functions or standing up additional services.

Narvar 决定把其基于微服务的平台迁移到 Apache Pulsar，是因为 Pulsar 与 Kafka 一样可靠、云中立且开源。而与 Kafka 不同的是，Pulsar 带来的维护开销极小，并且只需最少的人工干预即可扩展。Pulsar 从一开始就是容器化的、构建在 Kubernetes 之上，因而具备更强的可扩展性与可维护性。对 Narvar 而言最重要的是 Pulsar Functions：它使 Narvar 能够开发出直接在消息系统本身上消费并处理传入事件的微服务，从而不再需要昂贵的 Lambda 函数，也不必再额外搭建服务。

In a microservices architecture, each microservice is designed as an atomic and self-sufficient piece of software. These independent software components run as multiple processes distributed across multiple servers. A microservices-based application requires the interaction of multiple services via some sort of inter-process communication. The two most commonly used communication protocols are HTTP request/response and lightweight messaging. Pulsar was a perfect candidate for providing the lightweight messaging system that supports asynchronous messaging required by Narvar.

在微服务架构中，每个微服务都被设计为一个原子的、自给自足的软件单元。这些独立的软件组件以多个进程的形式运行，分布在多台服务器上。基于微服务的应用需要多个服务之间通过某种进程间通信进行交互。最常用的两种通信协议是 HTTP 请求/响应和轻量级消息传递。Pulsar 正是提供 Narvar 所需的、支持异步消息的轻量级消息系统的理想选择。
