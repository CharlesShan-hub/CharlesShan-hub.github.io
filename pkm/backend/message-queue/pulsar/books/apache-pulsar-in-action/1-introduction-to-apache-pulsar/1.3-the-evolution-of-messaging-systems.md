## 1.3 The evolution of messaging systems

Now that we have clearly defined what constitutes an EMS along with the core capabilities it provides, I would like to provide a brief historical review of messaging systems and how they have evolved over the years. Messaging systems have been around for decades and have been effectively used within many organizations, so Apache Pulsar isn’t some brand-new technology that emerged on the scene but rather another step in the evolution of the messaging system. By providing some historical context, my hope is that you will be able to understand how Pulsar compares to existing messaging systems.

既然我们已经清楚界定了什么构成 EMS、以及它提供哪些核心能力，接下来我想简要回顾一下消息系统的历史，以及它们这些年来是如何演进的。消息系统已经存在数十年之久，并在许多组织中得到有效使用，因此 Apache Pulsar 并非横空出世的全新技术，而是消息系统演进过程中的又一步。通过交代一些历史背景，我希望你能够理解 Pulsar 与现有消息系统相比处于什么位置。
