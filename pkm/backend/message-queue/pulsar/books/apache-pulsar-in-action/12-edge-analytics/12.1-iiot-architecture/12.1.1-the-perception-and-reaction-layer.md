### 12.1.1 The perception and reaction layer

The perception and reaction layer contains all of the hardware components (i.e., the things within the Internet of Things). As the basis for every IIoT system, these connected devices are responsible for perceiving the physical conditions of the industrial equipment and surrounding environment as sensed through numerous sensors that are either embedded in the devices themselves or implemented as standalone objects. These sensors emit a continuous, real-time stream of sensor readings either over lightweight machine-to-machine (LwM2M) protocols, such as Bluetooth, Zigbee, and Z-Wave, or longer-range protocols, such as MQTT or LoRa, if they have a wired connection.

感知与反应层包含了全部的硬件组件（也就是物联网中的那些"物"）。作为每个 IIoT 系统的基础，这些联网设备负责感知工业设备及其周边环境的物理状况——这种感知是通过众多传感器实现的，它们有的被嵌入设备自身内部，有的则作为独立的对象来实现。这些传感器会持续不断地发出实时的传感器读数流，其方式要么是经由轻量级的机器到机器（LwM2M）协议，例如蓝牙、Zigbee 和 Z-Wave；要么是经由更远距离的协议，例如 MQTT 或 LoRa（如果它们有有线连接的话）。

In practice, most IIoT environments require multiple network protocols to support the various devices within the environment. Battery-powered sensors, for example, can only communicate over short distances using lightweight protocols designed for short-range use. In general, the larger the distance the signal needs to travel, the more power that is required by the device to send it. Battery-operated devices using a longer-range protocol to send their data aren’t practical.

在实践中，大多数 IIoT 环境都需要多种网络协议，来支撑环境内的各种不同设备。举例来说，电池供电的传感器只能借助那些为短距离使用而设计的轻量级协议，在短距离内进行通信。一般而言，信号需要传播的距离越远，设备发送它所需的电量就越多。让电池供电的设备使用一个远距离协议去发送它们的数据，是不现实的。

One of the primary responsibilities of the perception and reaction layer is to perceive the environment via the sensors by capturing their readings and relaying them up to the data processing layer. The other critical responsibility of this layer is reacting to the actionable insights produced by the processing layer and translating them into an immediate physical action when we detect a potentially dangerous condition. Being able to detect a potentially dangerous condition doesn’t provide much business value if you can’t respond to it in a meaningful way.

感知与反应层的主要职责之一，是通过各个传感器来感知环境——采集它们的读数，并把这些读数向上转发给数据处理层。该层的另一项关键职责，是对处理层所产出的那些可付诸行动的洞察做出反应，并在我们探测到某种潜在危险状况时，把它们转化成即时的物理动作。如果无法以有意义的方式做出响应，那么仅仅能够探测到一种潜在危险状况，并不能带来多少业务价值。

Several decades before the emergence of the IIoT, most large manufacturers invested large amounts of time and effort into specialized software systems known as *supervisory control and data acquisition* (SCADA) systems that allow them to monitor and control their industrial equipment. These systems contain control networks that permit the automated operation of the mechanical or electro-mechanical devices known as *actuators* that can perform a variety of manual operations, such as opening a pressure valve on a piece of equipment or completely turning off the power to a given machine.

在 IIoT 出现的几十年前，大多数大型制造商就已经投入了大量的时间和精力，建设那些被称为*数据采集与监视控制*（supervisory control and data acquisition，SCADA）系统的专用软件系统，以便监控和控制自己的工业设备。这些系统中包含若干控制网络，它们让那些被称为*执行器*（actuator）的机械或机电设备得以自动化运作——这些执行器可以执行各种各样的手动操作，例如打开某台设备上的一个压力阀，或者彻底切断某台机器的电源。

By leveraging the existing control network within these SCADA systems, we are able to programmatically utilize these actuators from within our IIoT application by simply sending the correct command to activate the actuator. Where the sensors act as the “eyes” of this layer, the actuators serve an equally important role as the “hands” that allow us to respond to the data. Without them, our ability to react to a catastrophic sensor reading would be nonexistent. The information collected within the perception and reaction layer is sent over the *transportation layer* which, as the name implies, is responsible for the secure transmission of the sensor data up to a centralized data processing layer.

通过利用这些 SCADA 系统中现有的控制网络，我们就能够在自己的 IIoT 应用程序内以编程方式使用这些执行器——只需发送正确的命令来激活该执行器即可。如果说传感器充当着这一层的"眼睛"，那么执行器就扮演着同样重要的角色——它们是让我们能够对数据做出响应的"手"。没有它们，我们对一个灾难性传感器读数做出反应的能力将荡然无存。在感知与反应层内所采集到的信息，会通过*传输层*（transportation layer）发送出去；顾名思义，该层负责把传感器数据安全地传输到一个集中式的数据处理层。
